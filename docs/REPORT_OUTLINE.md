## §1 · Problem and strategy overview (~400 words)

**Lead with the rule, in two sentences.** Rank eligible stocks by 12-1 momentum; hold the
top 10 equal-weighted, rebalanced monthly. A reader must be able to reimplement it from
this paragraph alone — that is what guidelines §3 means by "systematic enough that another
evaluator can understand how the portfolio was produced".

**Then the central idea, which is not the momentum rule.** The idea is that a 10-stock book
out of ~190 has enormous luck-driven dispersion, so *any* result has to be measured against
what luck alone produces. State the headline result and the band together, never separately:
₹10,76,49,806, and 0 of 10,000 random 10-stock books beat it.

**Say what was deliberately excluded and why:** no optimiser, no risk overlay, no fitted
parameter. Under a raw-PNL metric, risk reduction is a handicap, and every avoided parameter
is a thing that cannot be overfitted.

*Sources:* `numbers.md` §1; `PROJECT.md` §3–§4.

## §2 · Data (~450 words)

Guidelines §9 asks for source, frequency, period, variables, and cleaning. Cover, in order:

- **Source and frequency.** Yahoo Finance via `yfinance`, daily OHLCV, 2019-06-03 →
  2026-08-24. Explain the 18-month pre-window buffer: the first rebalance needs a full
  252-day lookback, so data must start well before 2021.
- **Universe.** NSE-published Nifty 100 + Nifty Midcap 100 constituent lists, today's
  membership applied across the window per the organisers' clarification. 200 names.
- **The point-in-time reconstruction.** 27 dated NSE index-review press releases, committed
  in the repo, rebuilt by rolling today's list backwards under three invariants. **This is
  the strongest paragraph in the section** — say plainly that it exists to *measure* the
  bias the mandated universe carries, and forward-reference §7.
- **Cleaning, with the numbers.** 1,786-day calendar built as the union of days any name
  traded; 5 non-sessions removed; 3 corporate actions corrected against NSE records; 2
  demergers with no published ratio handled by selling before the ex-date; zero interior
  gaps in the final panel.
- **One sentence on what was checked and found wrong**, because it demonstrates the process:
  a single stale bar (2025-03-18) that Yahoo printed but NSE never traded shifted every
  positional lookback after it and was worth roughly half of an earlier stress-test result.

*Sources:* `data/reports/data_quality.md` (generated); `PROJECT.md` §13; `DECISIONS.md`
A8/A16/A17.

## §3 · Methodology (~650 words)

The four rules guidelines §3 asks for, each in its own short block:

1. **Selection.** 12-1 momentum: `P(t−21)/P(t−252) − 1`, computed through the close of
   t−1. Top 10 by rank. Eligibility requires a complete 252-day window, tradeable status
   from yesterday's volume, and index membership.
2. **Weighting.** Equal, 1/10, reset at each rebalance. Say why 1/N and not an optimiser:
   at 10 names, an estimated covariance matrix is noise, and the metric rewards return
   rather than efficiency.
3. **Rebalancing.** Monthly, first trading day. Costs of 0.1% on traded notional, both
   sides, charged on every execution including the initial build.
4. **Causality.** The one paragraph a quant panel will look for: the signal uses data
   strictly through t−1 and fills at t's open, and this is enforced by a test that scrambles
   all prices from the rebalance date onward and requires the book to be unchanged.

**Then the part that distinguishes this submission — how the configuration was chosen.**
Every axis was pre-registered with its predictions before it ran and adjudicated against a
band drawn beforehand: 8 cadence × weighting cells, 6 lookback × skip cells, a smallcap
universe arm, and 46 composite-signal arms. **62 configurations, and the simple rule won.**
Include the summary table from `PROJECT.md` §15. Mention that the losing arms are reported
with their failed predictions, because that is the claim being made about the process.

*Sources:* `PROJECT.md` §11 and §15; `DECISIONS.md` B1/B2/B3/C2-r/A19.

## §4 · Tools (~150 words)

Python 3.12; pandas, numpy, scipy, yfinance, pyarrow, matplotlib, pytest. No ML library is
used in the submitted strategy — say so explicitly, and note that the composite-signal and
diagnostic work used scikit-learn and was rejected on measurement. One line on
reproducibility: every parameter in `config.yaml`, every stochastic step seeded (20260824),
95 tests.

## §5 · Results and performance metrics (~700 words)

**Reproduce the guidelines §7 table verbatim from `numbers.md` §1** — total return,
annualised return, MDD, Sharpe, gain-to-loss, accuracy, trade statistics. Do not paraphrase
the numbers.

Then three things the table does not say:

- **Figure 1** (growth of ₹1 crore, log scale) with the benchmarks on the same axes.
- **Figure 3** (drawdown) — pair the −32.4% MDD with when it happened rather than leaving it

- **The band.** 0 of 10,000 random books beat the strategy; and at the **96th percentile per
  unit of risk**, this is not merely a volatility loading. Report both, and say why both are
  needed — the raw number answers "better than random?", the risk-adjusted one answers "or

**The out-of-sample window belongs here too:** +7.60% in H1 2026 against the Nifty 100's
−6.65%, at the 88.9th percentile of a fresh band. State that it was used **only** as a
one-way rejection filter and that no parameter was ever chosen by looking at it.

*Sources:* `numbers.md` §1, §3, §4; `composition.md` for holdings.

## §6 · Benchmark comparison (~350 words)

Guidelines §8 asks which benchmark and why. Use **both**, and justify the pair:

- **Nifty 100** — the mandate-facing comparison, cost-free by construction: +89.4%.
- **Equal-weight universe** — the honest one: the same 200 names, same dates, same costs,
  same engine, equally weighted. +280.6%. This is what separates *stock selection* from
  *being invested in a rising universe*, and it is the harder bar.

Say the quiet part: against the index the strategy is +987 pp, against its own equal-weight
universe +796 pp. The second number is the one that measures the strategy.

*Sources:* `numbers.md` §2; figure 1.

## §7 · Limitations and discussion (~450 words)

Lead with the largest, in the first sentence, unhedged:

1. **Index-inclusion bias, 488 percentage points.** The mandated universe uses today's
   constituents, so a stock is eligible in 2021 partly because it had risen enough to join
   the index by 2026. The identical rule point-in-time returns +388.0% against +876.5%.
   State that the repo runs both from one config word, and that the edge survives the
   correction — 9,996 of 10,000 random books still lose to it.
2. **Window specificity.** 2021–25 was exceptional for Indian mid-caps.
3. **Price return only** — roughly 9pp of median five-year return understated.
4. **Demergers exited, not corrected**; whole-share flooring on back-adjusted prices; one
   name excluded for a 41-session data hole.
5. **What would break it.** A momentum rule with no regime filter is exposed to a sharp
   trend reversal; the −32.4% drawdown is the visible cost, and it was accepted rather than
   engineered away because the metric is raw PNL.

**Close on the negative results, not the positive one.** Figure 4 (the cadence grid) and
figure 2 (the band) support the strongest available claim: 62 configurations were tested,
each pre-registered, and the simplest rule won. A strategy that has survived a search this
wide and been reported with its failures is a different object from one tuned until it
looked good.
